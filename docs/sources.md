## What counts as evidence here

**Observed:** what an accessible product page displayed, including variant and availability limits. A listed price is not a completed checkout quote.

**Brand-stated:** fabric, fit, manufacturing, certification, shrinkage, or performance language attributed to the seller. Listing these descriptions does not independently validate them.

**Interpretation:** the segment labels, positioning lessons, and recommended research priority. These are our analysis of this small sample.

**Hypothesis:** a possible audience, promise, brand character, or page behavior. None is a recorded user choice or a consumer finding.

**Still unknown:** demand, willingness to pay, market size, supplier economics, and our shirt's product specifications and performance. No customer interviews, purchases, or garment tests were performed for this desk research.

## Before making a commercial decision

Recheck the exact White variant, size, pack count, price, stock, taxes, and shipping. Obtain supplier quotes and real product evidence. Different fabric weights, silhouettes, colors, and discounts are not equivalent quality comparisons. A handful of brands does not establish a market gap or a suitable selling price.

The proposed [research plan](./research-plan.md) identifies the next evidence to collect. Local downloads, sample records, and working material belong in ignored `references/`; publish only selected, appropriate summaries in `docs/`.
