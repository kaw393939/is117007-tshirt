# The studio brief

One ordinary white T-shirt is the constant. Audience, meaning, voice, and presentation are the design decisions.

This hub prepares those decisions through competitor evidence and provisional concepts. Its editorial styling is a container for the research; it does not select the eventual T-shirt brand.

## What the reference project asks us to do

The [Brand Studio README](https://github.com/kaw393939/is117-brand-studio/blob/ca4b26431310a419e7143c56cacbc450c452879b/README.md) describes a deliberately neutral storefront and a cycle of **Try → Decide → Build → Check → Keep**. The [instructor notes](https://github.com/kaw393939/is117-brand-studio/blob/ca4b26431310a419e7143c56cacbc450c452879b/docs/instructor.md) leave the brand unfinished so the learner can make and explain the choices.

| Step | Useful outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Try | Explore the neutral storefront, size selector, and temporary demo bag. |
| Decide | Identify a primary audience situation and one contrasting secondary need. Draft a name, promise, one brand archetype, and three voice traits. |
| Compare | Inspect two small hero studies informed by real modernist and postmodernist references. Keep the shirt, headline, supporting copy, and action identical. |
| Build | Apply an original SVG logo, shared design tokens, and a consistent voice to the storefront and live style guide. Propose two truthful persuasion applications. |
| Check | Run the prepared checks, inspect rendered pages, explain what one actual check establishes, and collect real browser observations. |
| Keep | Save agreed choices and evidence, review the pull request, and decide whether to merge and publish. |

These steps summarize the reference exercise. They do not report completed work in the storefront.

## Keep the product boundary clear

The reference [SPEC](https://github.com/kaw393939/is117-brand-studio/blob/ca4b26431310a419e7143c56cacbc450c452879b/SPEC.md) supplies a white-shirt demonstration with a size selector and temporary bag. It does not establish fabric composition, weight, opacity, manufacturing origin, durability, environmental performance, price, delivery, or a guarantee. The demo does not take payments or real orders and has no customer accounts or persistent bag.

Competitor facts belong to the competitor being cited. A brand's cotton claim or fit photograph cannot become a claim about our shirt. Where the product is fictional, omit unsupported specifications or label illustrative content explicitly.

## Keep evidence honest

The reference [coaching guidance](https://github.com/kaw393939/is117-brand-studio/blob/ca4b26431310a419e7143c56cacbc450c452879b/AGENTS.md) distinguishes learner decisions, AI suggestions, actual checks, and unresolved gaps. If the storefront exercise begins later, preserve that distinction in its SPEC and review any AI-drafted learning excerpts before publication. This hub makes no claim about learner understanding or completed customer research.
